# Changelog

All notable changes to CBM Media Player are listed here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-05-06 — Initial Release

### Added
- Play every video format: MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, WEBM, WMV, OGG and more
- Full audio support: Dolby Atmos, DTS, TrueHD, AAC, FLAC
- DLNA casting to TV and projector
- Apple TV casting via macOS Screen Mirroring
- Embedded and external subtitle support (SRT, ASS, PGS and more)
- Library with video thumbnails, Continue Watching, Favorites
- Gradient accent themes
- Resume playback from where you left off
- Playlist support
- Picture-in-Picture and mini player
- Audio sync control and playback speed
